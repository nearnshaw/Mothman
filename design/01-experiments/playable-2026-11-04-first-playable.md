# First playable — exploratory build · 2026-11-04

**Route:** exploratory try (no registered hypothesis, no criterion). Owner asked: "make me a first playable demo of this idea."

## Intent

Make the GDD's core loop tangible: a dark forest, one flashlight, moths that orbit the beam and leave when it goes out, a Mothman that answers the light and not the player, and the telepathic reveal landing after a period of ignorant play. Everything is greybox: primitives, synthesized sound, no downloaded assets.

## Build (what exists)

- `scene.json`: fixed night time (1:30 am). **8 × 8 parcels (128 × 128 m)** since pass 2 (owner: many more, no limit). Everything is parameterized by scene size.
- `assets/scene/main.composite`: generated by `scripts/gen-forest.py` (scene closed in the Hub): ground, a faintly visible dirt road from the spawn corner to a concrete dome, 19 primitive trees with trunk colliders, a dead dog on the road. All registered in `inspector::Nodes`, names prefixed `Forest:`.
- `src/environment.ts`: black sky with faint stars, sun off, dense dark fog, first person forced over the whole play space.
- `src/flashlight.ts`: spotlight with shadows parented to the camera; **F toggles**. Tracks continuous and total lit time. Flicker is capped at 3 dips/s and never fully off (photosensitivity rule). Punishment can kill it for 6 s.
- `src/moths.ts`: 12-moth pool. In the beam they orbit a ring around the cone, with occasional stalls (climb and collapse). After 12 s of continuous beam, one moth every 4.5 s spirals into the circle of light and stays on the ground (up to 24 dead moths persist). Light off: every moth leaves in a straight line and vanishes.
- `src/npc/mothman/`: the HTN domain (`mothman.domain` → generated `mothman.htn.ts`), the primitive body (2 m body, gliding wings, red eyes with eyeshine only when the beam is on them) and the host (`host.ts`: facts, replan on fact-key change, executors, movement, sound).
- `src/telepathy.ts` + `src/hud.tsx`: the passage from GDD §10, one line at a time over ~37 s with a drone; an opening caption; the F hint; a "raise quality above Low" hint.
- `assets/audio/*.ogg`: wind, hum, screech, flutter, drone, click, whoosh. Synthesized with numpy; placeholders.

## The Mothman's decision (mothman.domain)

Branches in priority order: speak (when the host says the reveal is due) → flee the dark when close → slip away at mid range → lurk somewhere new in the dark → punish after the reveal (approach, circle, screech) → press after 8 s of beam (screech, approach near, loom with flicker) → stalk after 3 s (approach to mid range) → watch.

Reveal trigger (host): ignorant phase, beam on, and either 40 s total lit time with at least one dropped moth, or 75 s total. After the passage the phase is `revealed`; from then on a lit beam is answered immediately, and 18 s of lit time kills the flashlight for 6 s.

## Smoke

`npm run build`: type check and bundle pass; composite accepted (2026-11-04). **In-world smoke: not run.** Launching the Explorer through its MCP server needs the owner's go-ahead (the skill's intent gate), which an autonomous session cannot obtain. First run is the owner's, from the Creator Hub **Preview** (desktop, quality above Low). Nothing visual is verified yet: every claim about how it looks and feels is unverified.

## To enlarge the scene

1. Set `scene.parcels` in `scene.json` (e.g. 3 × 3).
2. Run `python3 scripts/gen-forest.py` with the scene closed in the Creator Hub.
3. `npm run build`. Distance bands and the dome position scale automatically.

## Known limits

- True darkness is desktop-only; the mobile app keeps its sky (night time still applies).
- On the desktop Low quality preset no scene light renders: the beam is invisible. The HUD hints at it; nothing else mitigates it yet.
- The Mothman is local to each client; nothing is synced. Other players' flashlights are not part of the design yet (open question, GDD §13).
- The HTN module is vendored at the pinned commit; `npm run build/start` regenerate the domain, the Creator Hub's Preview does not (run `npm run htn:generate` after editing the `.domain`).

**Still open after this pass:** the Mothman is still primitives (a modelled body needs Blender, which needs the owner's go-ahead to run);  the HUD paper panel was re-tuned for readability after the screenshot and is unverified since.
