# Audience and acquisition notes

Source: the owner's research sheet "CDS Deep Research Cryptozoology_Dark_Academia", Cryptozoology tab, read as CSV on 2026-11-04. It is a study of r/Cryptozoology and r/cryptids threads with "studio check" questions and research cautions. Each line below keeps the sheet's caution next to the finding. ## Two communities, two tones

- **r/Cryptozoology** rejects folklore beings being labelled cryptids and mocks sensational videos; a high-engagement thread threw out skinwalkers and wendigos as "not a cryptid". Humour is weaker here; evidence debate is the ritual (most-plausible-cryptid threads, thylacine, Orang Pendek, Patterson–Gimlin frame analysis).
- **r/cryptids** is playful: Fresno Nightcrawler "walking pants", "leggy bois", creatures as mascots.
- Caution from the sheet: do not assume one shared definition across them; do not caricature all participants as believers.
- **Implication for Mothman:** the owner's tone (scary, sad, guilt) belongs with the serious crowd. Mothman is accepted there as a named case, but note the folklore-vs-cryptid sensitivity: the game should not call him anything. Witness reports, newsprint and ambiguity are the right register; "monster" marketing is the wrong one.

## Demand and rivals

- A member asked for "a game involving a cryptid hunter in a forest"; replies named *BIGFOOT* and *Folklore Hunter*. Caution: do not say the genre has no games.
- An indie developer pitched a four-player hunt (inspect clues → identify → research habitat → bait/trap) with lesser-known creatures (Hidebehind, Snallygaster, Flatwoods Monster) instead of Bigfoot. The sheet asks whether players prefer observation, capture, escaping or fighting, and notes a developer proposal is not proof of demand.
- A creature-wishlist thread surfaced regional favourites (El Cuero, Mongolian Death Worm). Caution: duplicate cross-posts are not independent demand.
- Thylacine threads carry a conservation / respectful-search mood; the sheet asks whether "a respectful search theme could be more appealing than killing".
- **Implication:** the rivals are hunts. This game is the inverse: you are the lamp, not the hunter; the second half is a respectful retreat. That is the "exact missing activity" the sheet asks for, as a hypothesis to test, not a fact.

## Evidence culture (what reads as authentic)

- Grainy 16 mm footage dissected for walk cycle and camera angle; the staged Surgeon's Photograph; footprint casts, hair samples, field journals at the International Cryptozoology Museum; the Mothman Museum's clippings, photos and newspapers.
- Standing questions from the sheet: how to represent uncertain footage without taking a side; what details make an in-game sighting arguable; could new conflicting evidence change a group's mind.
- Cautions: do not reproduce copyrighted film frames or archival images without rights; museum presence does not validate authenticity; avoid claiming reported sightings are facts.
- **Implication:** keep every pre-reveal sighting arguable (owl, crane, heron, hoax balloon), use written newsprint rather than scanned archives, and consider the self-writing witness statement as the game's evidence artefact.

## Words that matter

- PGF / Patty (the 1967 Bigfoot film and its figure); "cryptid" vs "fearsome critter"; "not a cryptid".
- Avoid: taking Indigenous beliefs out of context (skinwalker, wendigo and, by extension, the Cornstalk curse).
