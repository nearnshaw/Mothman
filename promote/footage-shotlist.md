# Mothman: footage shot list

What to record in the Explorer for the footage posts and the Shorts, and how.

## Tips

- Required: one clean in-world 16:9 screenshot, 1920x1080 or larger, with no UI and no text, just gameplay with people playing.
- Recommended: 2-4 more 16:9 gameplay stills, at least 1920x1080, for social posts, the newsletter and press.
- Optional trailer: under 30 s, 16:9 1920x1080, with people in motion in the first three seconds.
- Trailer vertical cut: 9:16 1080x1920, under 30 s, for mobile and social.
- Trailer captions or on-screen text so it works with the sound off.
- Record in the Decentraland desktop Explorer at 1920x1080 with graphics quality above Low: on Low no scene light renders and the flashlight is invisible.
- Sound on, system audio recorded: the howl up the road, the TV whine when the beam stutters and his calls from where you are not looking carry every clip.
- He only notices the beam after about 18 seconds of it being on, and presses after 45. Hold the light to bring him; switch off to send him up and away.
- The cabin beat needs the bulb on with you inside for about half a minute before the window, a minute for the door. Switch the bulb off to end it.
- A death resets the whole night (the dog is back on the road, the papers unread). Use that for retakes of the opening.
- For vertical reels keep him in the middle third of the 16:9 frame; he stands where you are looking.
- Keep bunker 7, the voice and the walk back to the gate out of public clips. The reveal is the game.

## Shots

### Footage: the trailer cut  (`footage-01`)

30 seconds, 16:9, captions on. Shot order: (1) the gate from inside, beam off, stars, 3 s, card 'Point Pleasant, W.Va. November 1966'; (2) the beam clicks on at the paw prints on the road, moths arrive, 6 s; (3) the dead dog in the beam, pan up the empty road, 4 s, card 'Your dog went under the wire at dusk'; (4) the cabin window from outside with the bulb on, the figure at the glass, 4 s, card 'Something is at the window'; (5) two red eyes far down the road in the beam, the beam stutters, the TV whine, 5 s, card 'Something answers it'; (6) switch off: black, the moths leaving straight, 3 s; (7) the wordmark, 5 s. Nothing from bunker 7. The placeholder here is a teaser cut from the key art, not gameplay: replace it with a recording.

Caption: One flashlight. Something answers it.  [FOOTAGE]

Video in the kit: `videos/footage-01.mp4`. A recording from the Explorer still beats it: drop one in `promote/footage/` and ask for the video again.

### Footage: the moths leave straight  (`footage-02`)

First person on the road with the beam on for ten seconds so five or six moths are orbiting the cone. Hold the shot still, then press F. The moths leave in straight lines within a second. 8 to 10 seconds, cut one second after they are gone. Loops well; works vertical if the cone is centred.

Caption: Watch what they do when the light goes out.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: the window  (`footage-03`)

Inside the cabin with the bulb on, reading the plant map, about 30 seconds in. Turn to the nearest window as the bulb starts to flicker: his face at the glass, the tap on the window, the caption 'Something is at the window.' 6 to 8 seconds, cut on the tap. Keep the light switch and what happens after off screen.

Caption: The bulb is on. It can see the bulb.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: eyeshine  (`footage-04`)

On the road with the beam on, sweep slowly left to right across the trees at mid distance. Two red points light up where the beam passes and go dark when it moves on. 5 to 7 seconds. Do not approach; the shot ends before he moves. Best vertical with the eyes in the middle third.

Caption: Two red reflectors, forty yards out. Your flashlight was on at the time.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: the road is empty  (`footage-05`)

Walk up to the dead dog on the road, hold the beam on it for three seconds, walk ten metres on, turn around: the road is empty, the caption 'Something took it while your back was turned.' 10 to 12 seconds. Cut on the caption.

Caption: You look back for the dead dog.  [FOOTAGE]

Keep the window in the middle third of the 16:9 recording. Nothing of the door or what follows.

### Short: the light is the problem  (`short-02`)  · vertical 9:16, a Short

Vertical. Beam on down the road, the hook card over the first 2 seconds; a count-up caption as the seconds pass; the two red eyes; the beam stutters with the TV whine; he is suddenly at mid distance; press F: black, wings going up. 20 to 30 seconds. Keep the road centred for the 9:16 crop.

Hook on screen 0-2 s: This horror game punishes you for keeping the light on.
Beats: 18 seconds: he notices → 45 seconds: he comes → the beam stutters → not a battery problem → put it out
